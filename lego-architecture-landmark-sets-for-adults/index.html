How to pick the right LEGO landmark

Match the set to the person and the space:

  • Pick a place that means something: their honeymoon city, a bucket-list trip or where they grew up beats the biggest box.
  • Measure first: the Colosseum, Shuttle Carrier Aircraft and Concorde need a lot of room. Paris City of Love hangs on a wall, and Tokyo fits on a desk.
  • New to LEGO? Tokyo, the Statue of Liberty or Himeji Castle are relaxed first builds. The Colosseum is a months-long project.
  • Love surprises? The Great Pyramid, Taj Mahal and Notre-Dame have removable parts that reveal the inside.
  • Check the seller: retired sets like the Colosseum and Boutique Hotel come from third-party sellers, so tap through and check today's price on Amazon.
Gift tip: tuck a photo from their trip to that place into the box, so the finished model becomes a souvenir.
