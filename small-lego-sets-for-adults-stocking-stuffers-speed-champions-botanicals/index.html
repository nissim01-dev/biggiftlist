How to pick a small LEGO gift for an adult

A few quick tips before you buy:

  • Don't worry about the age on the box: Speed Champions and Creator sets say 8+ to 10+, but adult fans buy them for quick builds and desk displays.
  • Collectible lines win: the F1 Speed Champions cars and Botanicals plants look better in groups, so one set often starts a collection.
  • Match their passion: cars for car people, flowers for plant lovers, Dobby for Harry Potter fans.
  • Check the seller: most picks are sold by Amazon.com. Dobby comes from a third-party seller shipped by Amazon, so check today's price and the return policy.
