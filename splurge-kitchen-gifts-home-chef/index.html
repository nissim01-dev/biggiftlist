How to pick a big kitchen gift

  • Check counter space first. Countertop ovens, bowl-lift mixers and compressor ice cream makers are big. A gift that has to live in the garage rarely gets used.
  • Upgrade what they already use. Someone who makes smoothies daily will love a Vitamix; a toaster-oven user will love the Breville.
  • Prefer long track records. Big appliances with thousands of reviews and long warranties are safer bets than brand-new models.
  • Buy from Amazon when possible. Items shipped and sold by Amazon are simpler to return after the holidays.
Pair it: a KitchenAid pairs well with a baking cookbook, and a Vitamix with a soup cookbook. Small add-ons make a big gift feel personal.

FAQ

What is the best splurge kitchen gift for someone who cooks a lot?

A premium countertop oven like the Breville Smart Oven Air Fryer Pro gets used almost daily, which is why it tops many reviewers' lists.

Is a Vitamix worth the price?

For people who blend often, yes: Vitamix blenders are known for smooth results, the ability to heat soup and long warranties.

What's the difference between compressor and freezer-bowl ice cream makers?

Compressor machines (Whynter, Cuisinart no-pre-freeze) chill themselves and can run anytime. Freezer-bowl machines need the bowl frozen overnight.

Are these kitchen gifts good for a wedding or housewarming too?

Yes. Stand mixers, Vitamix blenders and countertop ovens are among the most popular registry items.
