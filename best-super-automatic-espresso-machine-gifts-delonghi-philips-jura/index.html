How to pick a super-automatic espresso machine gift

Match the machine to how they drink coffee:

  • Latte every day: pick a machine with an automatic milk system (Philips LatteGo or De'Longhi LatteCrema). The LatteGo carafe is the easiest to clean.
  • Iced coffee and cold brew fans: the De'Longhi Magnifica Duo and PrimaDonna Aromatic have cold brew and cold foam built in.
  • Espresso and black coffee only: a simpler machine like the Magnifica Start or Jura ENA 4 is all they need.
  • Two coffee drinkers: user profiles (Philips 5500) or two bean hoppers (De'Longhi Rivelia) keep everyone happy.
  • Prefer to pull shots by hand? A semi-automatic machine may suit them better. See our sister site Cozy Cup Corner.
Gift tip: wrap the machine with a bag of fresh whole beans from a local roaster and two nice cups, so the first latte happens on Christmas morning.

FAQ

What is a super-automatic espresso machine?

A machine that grinds, doses, tamps, brews and (on most models) froths milk with one button. No barista skills needed.

Philips, De'Longhi or Jura?

Philips LatteGo machines are the easiest to clean. De'Longhi offers the widest range, including cold brew. Jura machines are Swiss-made premium machines known for build quality.

Is a super-automatic worth it?

For someone who buys café drinks most days, a machine that makes them at home at a touch can pay for itself over time and gets used daily.

Can these make iced coffee?

Several can: the Philips 4400 and 5500, De'Longhi Magnifica Duo and PrimaDonna Aromatic, and the Ninja AutoBarista have iced or cold brew drinks.
