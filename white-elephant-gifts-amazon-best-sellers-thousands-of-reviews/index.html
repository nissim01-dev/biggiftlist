How to win the white elephant swap

A few quick tips before you buy:

  • Funny plus useful wins: the burrito blanket and the shark hoodie get a laugh and still get used every night. Pure jokes get left behind.
  • Go broad: you don't know who will open it, so pick things that work for anyone, like a candle warmer, a party game or a kitchen gadget.
  • Think about the room: keep it clean for office swaps. Save the louder gag gifts for friends and family.
  • Check the seller and the limit: several picks come from brand stores shipped by Amazon. Check today's price against your group's limit before you order.
