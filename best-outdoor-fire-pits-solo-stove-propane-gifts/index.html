How to pick the right fire pit gift

  • Propane vs wood: Propane lights fast, makes no ash and works on apartment patios where wood burns are restricted. Wood smokeless pits (Solo Stove, Breeo) feel more traditional and smell like a campfire.
  • Size: 15–19 inches for balconies and couples; 24–28 inches for family patios; Yukon/Breeo sizes for parties.
  • Fire table vs bowl: Tables double as furniture with a lid. Bowls are cheaper and easier to carry camping.
  • Surfaces: Use a stand, mat or non-flammable pad on decks. Keep clearances from siding and low branches.
  • Local rules: Some cities ban wood fires on dry days. Propane is the safer gift when rules are unclear.
Gift tip: wrap a propane pit with a full propane tank and a bag of marshmallow sticks, or wrap a Solo Stove with a bundle of kiln-dried firewood and a lid so it is ready for Christmas evening.

FAQ

What is the best outdoor fire pit to gift for Christmas 2026?

For most patios, the Outland Living 19-inch propane pit (easy, no ash) or the Solo Stove Bonfire with stand (iconic smokeless wood). Pick Ninja Fireside360 if they want a modern propane heater-fire hybrid.

Propane or wood-burning fire pit?

Choose propane for apartments, HOA rules, or zero ash. Choose a smokeless wood pit like Solo Stove or Breeo for the campfire experience with much less smoke than a ring of stones.

Is a Solo Stove worth it as a gift?

Yes if they have outdoor space and will burn dry wood. The secondary combustion design cuts smoke dramatically versus a basic steel ring. For no-maintenance flame, give propane instead.

What size fire pit should I buy?

15-inch Ranger for portability, 19.5-inch Bonfire for most patios, 27-inch Yukon or a 43-inch propane table for big gatherings.

Do I need a stand or mat?

Yes on wood decks and composite patios. Heat can scorch surfaces. Many Solo Stove listings include a stand; add one if it does not.
