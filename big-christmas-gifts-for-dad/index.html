How to pick a big gift for dad

  • Match the hobby, then upgrade it. A golfer will use a premium rangefinder every round; a dad who cooks will use a great countertop oven every week.
  • Smart telescopes suit busy dads. They work from the driveway in minutes, even in suburbs, and produce photos he can share.
  • Consider the space. Telescopes and countertop ovens need a home; robot vacuums need a dock spot.
  • Prefer Amazon-shipped listings for easier holiday returns and gift receipts.
Make it personal: pair a telescope with a printed star map, or a rangefinder with a round at his favorite course.
