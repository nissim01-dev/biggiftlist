How to pick a LEGO Lord of the Rings or fantasy set

A few quick tips before you buy:

  • Measure the shelf: Rivendell is about 28 inches wide and Barad-dûr is about 33 inches tall. Sauron's Helmet, the Balrog Book Nook and the Fell Beast fit on a desk or a bookshelf.
  • Build a collection: Rivendell, Barad-dûr and Minas Tirith are designed as big display pieces from the same films, so they make a great set over a few Christmases.
  • Check the seller: a few picks, like the Medieval Blacksmith, Hocus Pocus cottage and Fell Beast, come from third-party sellers. Check the seller, ratings and return policy before buying.
  • Match the fan: book lovers usually love the Balrog Book Nook, movie fans love the big centerpieces, and fantasy fans outside Tolkien may prefer Toothless or the medieval village.
Gift tip: pair a big Lord of the Rings set with a copy of the books or the extended-edition movies for a full Middle-earth gift.
