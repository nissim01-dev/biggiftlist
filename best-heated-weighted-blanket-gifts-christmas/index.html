How to pick a heated or weighted blanket gift

A few quick tips before you buy:

  • Heated vs weighted: heated throws warm cold hands and drafty rooms. Weighted blankets add gentle pressure for wind-down and sleep. Some people love both.
  • Throw or full-size: a 50x60 throw is perfect for the couch. Choose full-size when the gift is meant for the bed.
  • Weighted sizing: a common starting point is about 10 percent of body weight. 15 lb fits many adults; 20 lb is better for larger frames.
  • Safety first: heated blankets should have auto shut-off. Check today's price, heat settings and the return window before you order.
Gift tip: the Bedsure heated throws and the top weighted blankets move thousands of units a month in November and December. Popular colors sell out before Christmas week, so order early.

FAQ

Is a heated blanket a good Christmas gift?

Yes, especially for someone who is always cold. Look for auto shut-off, multiple heat levels and a brand with thousands of reviews.

What size weighted blanket should I buy?

A common rule of thumb is about 10 percent of the person's body weight. 15 lb queen blankets fit many adults; choose 20 lb for larger adults.

What is the difference between a heated throw and a weighted blanket?

A heated throw plugs in and warms you. A weighted blanket uses glass-bead fill for gentle pressure and does not heat unless it is a separate heated model.

Are heated blankets safe?

Modern heated throws with auto shut-off and overheat protection are widely used. Follow the maker's wash and use instructions and never fold a heated blanket tightly while it is on.
