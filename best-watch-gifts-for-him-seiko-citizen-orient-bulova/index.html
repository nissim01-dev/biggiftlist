How to pick a watch gift for him

A few quick tips before you buy:

  • Automatic or solar: automatic watches (Seiko, Orient, Bulova Sutton, Timex) wind themselves from wrist movement and need to be worn or wound. Citizen Eco-Drive watches charge from any light and never need a battery.
  • Check his wrist size: dress watches around 38–41 mm suit most wrists. Big chronographs at 44–45 mm look best on larger wrists.
  • Match his style: a dive or field watch for weekends and outdoors, a dress watch for the office and weddings, and a chronograph or GMT for gadget lovers and travelers.
  • Check the seller: some Seiko and Orient watches are sold by third-party dealers. Check the seller, ratings and return policy before buying.
Gift tip: add a second strap, like a leather or NATO strap in his size, so he can change the look of the watch for work and weekends.

FAQ

What is a good first automatic watch for a man?

The Seiko 5 Sports and the Orient Bambino are two of the most recommended first automatic watches. The Bulova Sutton adds an open window on the dial that shows the movement.

Do Citizen Eco-Drive watches need batteries?

No. Eco-Drive watches charge from any light, natural or artificial, so they never need a regular battery change.

What is the Bulova Lunar Pilot?

It's Bulova's tribute to the chronograph worn on the Moon during the Apollo 15 mission, with a high-frequency quartz movement for a smooth-sweeping chronograph seconds hand.

Which watch is best for a traveler?

A GMT watch like the Seiko 5 Sports GMT or a dual-time pilot watch like the Citizen Promaster Nighthawk lets him track a second time zone.
