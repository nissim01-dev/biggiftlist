How to pick the right LEGO flower set

Match the set to the person and the room:

  • Want a finished centerpiece? The Flower Arrangement and the Hibiscus include a buildable vase or pot. Most bouquets don't include a vase, so add one they'll love.
  • Romantic gift: the Bouquet of Roses (red) or Pink Roses. Both come with baby's breath.
  • Plant lover: the Orchid, Peace Lily or one of the bonsai trees look great on a desk or windowsill.
  • Go big: combine the Flower Bouquet and the Wildflower Bouquet in one large vase for a showpiece.
  • Not a flower person? The Insect Collection gives nature lovers a butterfly, a beetle and a mantis instead.
Gift tip: wrap the box with a real vase (or a simple glass jar and ribbon), so the bouquet can go on display the minute it's built.

What is the best LEGO flower set for adults?
