How to pick the right Instant Pot or multicooker

  • Pressure cooker or slow cooker? Pressure cookers (Instant Pot, Ninja HyperHeat, CHEF iQ) cook stews, beans and roasts fast. Slow-cooker-style multicookers (Ninja PossibleCooker, Crock-Pot, GreenPan) sear and slow cook but don't pressure cook.
  • Size: 6 quarts serves about 4 to 6 people and suits most homes. Choose 8 quarts or more for big families, batch cooking and holiday hosting.
  • Want crispy food? A combo with an air-fryer lid, like the Duo Crisp, replaces a separate air fryer for smaller batches.
  • Smart features: app control and built-in scales help beginners, but the basic Instant Pot Pro does everything most cooks need.
  • Pot material: stainless steel pots last for years and sear well; ceramic nonstick pots clean easier but need gentler utensils.
  • Buy from Amazon: every pick here is sold by Amazon.com, which makes holiday returns and gift receipts easier.

FAQ

What is the best Instant Pot to gift in 2026?

For most cooks, the Instant Pot Pro 6-quart: 10 functions including sous vide, a stovetop-friendly pot with handles and a gentle steam release. For crispy food too, the Duo Crisp 8-quart adds an air-fryer lid.

Instant Pot or Ninja: which multicooker is better?

Instant Pot has the largest range of pressure cookers and the most recipes online. Ninja's HyperHeat is a strong pressure cooker with a PFAS-free nonstick pot, and the PossibleCooker is a big slow-cooker-style multicooker for searing and hosting, not pressure cooking.

Is a pressure cooker with an air fryer worth it?

If counter space is tight, yes. The Duo Crisp pressure cooks and then air fries in the same base. If they already own a big air fryer, a plain Instant Pot Pro is the simpler gift.

What size Instant Pot should I buy?

6 quarts is right for most households of 2 to 6 people. Choose 8 quarts for larger families or batch cooking.

Is the Ninja PossibleCooker a pressure cooker?

No. It sears, slow cooks, braises, bakes and steams in an oven-safe pot, but it doesn't pressure cook. For pressure cooking from Ninja, choose the HyperHeat.
