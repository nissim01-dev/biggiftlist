Vertuo or Original? How to pick the right Nespresso

Ask one question first: do they drink big mugs or small espressos?

  • Vertuo reads a barcode on each capsule and brews everything from an espresso to a large mug or carafe, with a thick crema. Best for American-style coffee drinkers and iced coffee. Vertuo capsules come from Nespresso (including Starbucks by Nespresso Vertuo pods).
  • Original is built for true espresso and lungo shots. Best for espresso, cappuccino and latte lovers, and it has the widest capsule choice, including compatible pods from other brands.
  • Milk drinks? Pick a Lattissima (built-in milk system) or a bundle with the Aeroccino frother. If they already own a machine, the Aeroccino 3 alone is a great gift.
  • Small kitchen: the Vertuo Pop+, Essenza Mini and Pixie are the most compact here.
  • Third-party sellers: some Original-line machines are sold by third-party sellers. Check the seller and the return policy before buying.

FAQ

Which is better, Nespresso Vertuo or Original?

Vertuo is better for big mugs and iced coffee; Original is better for true espresso and milk drinks, with more capsule choices. Pick by how they drink coffee.

What is the best Nespresso machine for lattes?

The Vertuo Lattissima for Vertuo drinkers, or the Gran Lattissima for Original espresso drinks. Both make milk drinks at one touch.

Can Vertuo machines use Original capsules?

No. Each system uses its own capsules, so buy capsules that match the machine.

Is a Nespresso a good gift for someone who already has a coffee maker?

Yes. It's a fast, no-mess way to make espresso and lattes that a drip machine can't. For a bigger upgrade, see our super-automatic espresso machine guide.
