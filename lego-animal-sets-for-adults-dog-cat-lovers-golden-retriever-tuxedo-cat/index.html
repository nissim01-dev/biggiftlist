How to pick the right LEGO animal set

A few quick tips before you buy:

  • Match the pet: a golden retriever owner will love the Icons puppy, and a black-and-white cat owner will love the Tuxedo Cat. Personal beats generic.
  • Display or play: Icons, Ideas and Art sets are display pieces for adults. Creator 3 in 1 sets are simpler and great for building with kids.
  • Pair big and small: a display set plus a small Creator animal makes a great main gift and stocking stuffer combo.
  • Check the seller: most picks are sold by Amazon.com, but a few come from third-party sellers shipped by Amazon. Check today's price and the return window before you order.
Gift tip: Snoopy's Doghouse and the Golden Retriever Puppy are selling thousands of units a month. Popular LEGO sets often go out of stock before Christmas, so order early.
