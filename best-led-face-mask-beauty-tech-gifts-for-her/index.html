How to pick a beauty tech gift

A few quick tips before you buy:

  • Mask or handheld: LED masks are hands-free, so she can relax or read during a session. Handheld devices like the medicube Booster need a few minutes of active use but are easier to travel with.
  • Hard or soft mask: hard-shell masks (Shark, iRestore, Ulike) hover over the skin. Flexible silicone masks like the INIA GLOW hug the face and feel lighter.
  • Consistency matters: most LED and microcurrent-style devices are designed for several sessions a week over a few weeks, so they suit someone who already enjoys a skincare routine.
  • Check with a dermatologist first if she has sensitive skin, a skin condition, light sensitivity or is pregnant, and always follow the brand's instructions.
Gift tip: pair a device with a cozy robe or a favorite serum or moisturizer to make it a full at-home spa gift.
