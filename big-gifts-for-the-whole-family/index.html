How to choose a gift the whole family will use

  • Think about shared moments. Movie nights, weekend breakfasts and clear nights outside are where family gifts shine.
  • Kid-friendly but grown-up quality. The best family gifts are simple enough for kids yet good enough that parents enjoy them too.
  • Check space and noise. Slushie and ice cream machines are big and can be loud; plan where they'll live.
  • Split the cost. Extended family can chip in on a bigger gift like a telescope or an Echo Show 15.
Make it an event: plan a 'first slushie' movie night or a 'first light' stargazing night between Christmas and New Year's.
