How to pick the right hair styler gift

  • Match the hair type: fine and straight hair holds air-styled curls less well; curly and coily hair benefits from a diffuser; thick hair needs a powerful dryer.
  • Curls, sleek or volume? Auto-wrap curlers for curls, ceramic or air straighteners for sleek looks, oval brushes for volume.
  • Air vs heat: air stylers use less direct heat than curling irons, but results last longer with a little heat, which is why the Glam and FlexFusion add ceramic.
  • Check the attachments: different editions of the same styler come with different attachments. Compare before you buy.
  • Check the seller: look for "Ships from and sold by Amazon.com" or the brand's own store for easier holiday returns.
Gift tip: add a heat-protectant spray, a silk scrunchie set and a storage case or stand made for the styler, and it becomes a full "salon at home" gift.

FAQ

What is the best Dyson Airwrap alternative on Amazon?

The Shark FlexStyle is the best-selling Airwrap-style styler on Amazon. It dries, curls and smooths, and it twists into a full hair dryer, typically for much less than an Airwrap.

Is the Shark FlexStyle good for curly hair?

Yes, especially the FlexStyle bundle with the curl-defining diffuser (HD435), which dries curls without blowing them into frizz.

Which hair styler is best for fine or straight hair?

Fine, straight hair holds curls less well with air alone. A styler that adds ceramic heat, like the Shark FlexStyle Glam or FlexFusion, tends to give longer-lasting results.
