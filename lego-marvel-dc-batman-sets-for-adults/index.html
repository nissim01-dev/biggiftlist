How to pick the right LEGO superhero set

Match the set to the fan and the shelf:

  • Biggest wow: the Daily Bugle or Avengers Tower are tall skyscrapers full of minifigures, so measure the shelf height first.
  • Batman fans: pick by movie: the Tumbler for the Nolan trilogy, the Batwing for the 1989 film.
  • Office or desk: the Infinity Gauntlet, Venom mask or Iron Man Mark 3 fit where a huge set won't.
  • Wall space, not shelf space: the Batwing mounts on a wall, and the Iron Man LEGO Art mosaic hangs like a picture.
  • Check the seller: several retired sets are sold by third-party sellers, so tap through and check today's price on Amazon.
Gift tip: pair a big set with a small display case or LED light kit, so the finished build becomes the room's centerpiece.
