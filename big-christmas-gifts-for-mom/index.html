Tips for choosing a big gift for mom

  • Give back time. Robot vacuum and mop combos consistently rank among the most appreciated household gifts.
  • Go sentimental. A photo frame pre-loaded with family pictures often becomes her favorite gift.
  • Pick her color. KitchenAid mixers and Our Place ovens come in many colors; matching her kitchen makes it feel personal.
  • Avoid 'chore' gifts she didn't ask for. If in doubt, pair a practical gift with something fun.
Family gift idea: siblings can split a bigger gift, like a KitchenAid plus the pasta roller attachment, or a robot vacuum plus a photo frame.
