How to pick a home bar gift

A few quick tips before you buy:

  • Cocktails or whiskey: cocktail fans love machines, smokers and bar kits. Whiskey drinkers usually prefer great glasses, a decanter and clear ice.
  • Counter space: the Margaritaville and the soda makers live on the counter. Smokers, glasses and bar kits store easily.
  • Smokers: handheld guns like the Breville need a dome or cover for drinks, while the smoking box and the whiskey smoker kit work on their own. Torch kits usually need butane, sold separately.
  • Zero-proof friends: sparkling water makers and smokers work just as well for mocktails, iced tea and lemonade.
Gift tip: pair a smoker or decanter with a bottle of their favorite spirit or a good bitters set for a ready-made home bar night.

What is the best gift for someone who has a home bar?
