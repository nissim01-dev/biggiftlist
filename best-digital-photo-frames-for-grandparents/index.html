Aura vs Skylight vs Frameo: which is easiest for grandparents?

  • Aura: family members add photos from the Aura app; the frame has no touchscreen, so there's nothing for grandparents to figure out. Free unlimited storage. Often called the best frame for gifting.
  • Skylight: anyone can email photos to the frame's address, which is perfect for relatives who don't want another app. The touchscreen lets grandparents swipe and react. Some extras need a subscription.
  • Frameo-based frames: budget touchscreen frames that use the free Frameo app. Great value, but lower resolution and generic builds.
  • Echo Show: a smart display rather than a pure frame. Adds video calling and a calendar, but needs an Amazon account to set up.
  • Set it up before you wrap it: connect it to Wi-Fi at the grandparents' house (or pre-load photos) so it works the moment it's unwrapped.
Gift tip: before Christmas, ask everyone in the family to send 10 favorite photos to the frame so it's already full of memories on the big day.

FAQ

What is the easiest digital photo frame for seniors?

Aura frames need nothing from the grandparent: the family adds photos from the app. Skylight is easiest for relatives because photos can be sent by email.

Do digital photo frames need Wi-Fi?

Wi-Fi frames (Aura, Skylight, Frameo, Nixplay) need Wi-Fi to receive new photos. Some can show photos already stored on the frame without a connection.

Aura or Skylight: which is better?

Aura is generally praised for image quality and design; Skylight wins for its email-to-frame feature and touchscreen. Both are top gifts.

Is there a monthly fee for digital photo frames?

Aura and Frameo frames work without a subscription. Skylight and Nixplay offer optional paid plans for extras like video or more storage.
