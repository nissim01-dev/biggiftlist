How to pick a LEGO movie or TV set

A few quick tips before you buy:

  • Start with their favorite: the best LEGO fan gift is the one tied to the movie or show they rewatch the most. Sitcom sets are full of inside jokes that only real fans will catch.
  • Check the shelf: the Black Pearl, the ECTO-1 and the T. rex skeleton are long display models. Seinfeld, Central Perk and E.T. fit on a regular bookshelf.
  • Retired sets and sellers: Central Perk, Seinfeld, The Office and the ECTO-1 are retired and now sold by third-party sellers. Check the seller, ratings and return policy before buying.
  • Match the age: most sets here are designed for adults, while the Jurassic Park Visitor Center (12+) and Morticia's Cottage (10+) are great for teens and building together.
Gift tip: pair a LEGO movie set with the film on Blu-ray or a streaming gift card for a ready-made movie night.
