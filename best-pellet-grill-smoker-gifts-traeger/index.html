How to pick the right pellet grill or smoker gift

  • Woodfire vs traditional pellets: Ninja Woodfire is electric and uses a small scoop of pellets for smoke flavor — easy on patios with an outlet. Cuisinart / Freedom / ONLYFIRE burn pellets as the main fuel like a classic pellet grill.
  • Portable vs backyard: Portable pellets and AKORN Jr. fit RVs and small patios. Barrel pellets and 30-inch vertical smokers need more floor space.
  • Electric vertical smokers: Masterbuilt and EAST OAK are set-and-forget chip smokers. They will not sear like a hot pellet grill, but they shine for ribs, turkey and overnight cooks.
  • Traeger / Pit Boss full grills: Many Amazon searches currently show covers, liners and controllers. If a complete Pro/Ironwood or Competition grill is in stock locally, buy there; on Amazon, prefer the complete cookers listed here and add Traeger pellets.
  • Fuel gift wrap: Always include pellets or chips so the cooker is usable on Christmas Day.
Gift tip: wrap a Woodfire or pellet grill with an 18 lb bag of Traeger Signature Blend pellets and a simple rub sampler, or pair a Masterbuilt with wood chips and a leave-in meat thermometer.

FAQ

What is the best pellet grill to gift on Amazon for Christmas 2026?

For most Amazon shoppers, a Ninja Woodfire (ProConnect XL or OG701) or the Cuisinart portable pellet grill. Full-size Traeger Pro models are often sold through other retailers when Amazon stock is accessories-only.

Is a Ninja Woodfire a real pellet grill?

It is an electric outdoor grill that burns a small amount of wood pellets for real smoke flavor. It will not replace a 500+ sq in Traeger for huge cookouts, but it is one of the best-selling smoke-flavor gifts on Amazon.

Pellet grill or electric smoker?

Choose a pellet / Woodfire grill for searing plus smoke. Choose a Masterbuilt or EAST OAK vertical smoker for easy low-and-slow with wood chips and less learning curve.

What size smoker should I buy?

Portables for tailgates and two people; Woodfire XL or a 465 sq in barrel for family weekends; 30-inch vertical electrics for ribs, butts and holiday turkeys.

What should I wrap with a smoker gift?

Pellets or chips, a leave-in probe thermometer and a bottle of BBQ sauce or rub. The Traeger Signature Blend bag is the easiest Amazon add-on.
