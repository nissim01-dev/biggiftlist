How to pick the right luggage gift

Think about how they travel before you think about the brand:

  • Carry-on only: most US airlines allow about 22 x 14 x 9 inches including wheels and handles. Check the airline's rules, especially for international flights.
  • Hardside or softside: hard shells protect fragile items and wipe clean; softside bags have outside pockets and squeeze into tight bins.
  • Weight: for older travelers or anyone who lifts their own bag, the Travelpro Maxlite models are among the lightest.
  • Warranty: Briggs & Riley, Travelpro, Samsonite, Away and Bellroy all offer long coverage. Register the bag after the gift is opened.
  • Color: many listings come in several colors. A bright color is easier to spot on the carousel.
Gift tip: tuck a luggage tag and a note about the next trip inside the suitcase. It turns a practical gift into a plan.

FAQ

What is the best carry-on luggage to give as a gift?

For a splurge, the TUMI Alpha Dual Access or the Briggs & Riley Baseline Essential. For a stylish hard shell, the Away Carry-On. For frequent flyers, the Travelpro Platinum Elite.

Is hardside or softside luggage better?

Hardside protects fragile items and is easy to clean. Softside is lighter in many cases and has outside pockets. Frequent flyers often prefer softside; vacation travelers often prefer hardside.

What is the lightest carry-on on this list?

The Travelpro Maxlite 5 softside spinner at about 5.4 lb.

Will these fit in the overhead bin?

The carry-ons here are designed for most US airline bins. The Away Bigger Carry-On and expanded bags may exceed some airlines' limits, so check before flying.
