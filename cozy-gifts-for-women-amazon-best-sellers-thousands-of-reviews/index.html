How to build a cozy gift she will love

A few quick tips before you buy:

  • Think in a bundle: one blanket or slipper as the main gift, plus socks, a lip balm or eye patches as small extras, makes a gift basket that looks far bigger than it was.
  • Size-free is safer: blankets, wearable blankets, pillowcases and candles never need a size. For slippers, check the size chart in the listing.
  • Match her routine: always cold? heated throw. Works from home? cloud slippers. Big on skincare? satin pillowcase and eye patches.
  • Check the seller: some picks are sold by Amazon.com and others by brand stores shipped by Amazon. Check today's price and the return window before you order.
