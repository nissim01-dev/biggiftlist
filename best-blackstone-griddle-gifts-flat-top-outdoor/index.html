How to pick a griddle gift

A few quick tips before you buy:

  • Size for the crowd: 36-inch for big families and parties, 28-inch for four to six people, 22-inch or 17-inch for couples, camping and tailgates.
  • Hood or no hood: a hood keeps rain and dust off the cooktop and helps melt cheese. Without one, add a cover.
  • Propane: tabletop models run on small 1 lb bottles; stand models use a standard propane tank, which is usually not included.
  • Cold climate: an indoor electric griddle like the Ninja Sizzle keeps the smash burgers going all winter.
  • Already own one? a tool kit or a fitted cover is the easy, always-useful add-on.
Gift tip: bundle a griddle with a tool kit and a bottle of griddle seasoning. It's ready for its first cookout the day it's unboxed.
