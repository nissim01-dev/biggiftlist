How to pick the right LEGO Disney set

Match the set to the fan and the space they have:

  • Huge shelf, huge fan: the Disney Castle is the ultimate build. Snow White's Cottage and the Villains Icons are big but easier to place.
  • Pixar over princesses? WALL-E and EVE, Luxo Jr. and the Up House are the picks, and all three are sold by Amazon.com.
  • Desk or office: Luxo Jr., Stitch and Sally's Flowerpot are compact and full of personality.
  • Building with kids: the Up House, Stitch and Sally's Flowerpot are rated 9+, while the 18+ sets are made for adult builders.
  • Third-party sellers: some retired Disney 100 sets are now sold mostly by third-party sellers. Check the seller and the return policy before buying.
Gift tip: pair the set with a movie night: wrap the box with a copy of the film, so the build and the watch happen the same weekend.
