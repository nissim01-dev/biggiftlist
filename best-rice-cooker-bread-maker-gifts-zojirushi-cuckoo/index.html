How to pick a rice cooker or bread maker gift

Match the machine to how they cook:

  • Fuzzy logic vs. induction: fuzzy-logic cookers adjust time and temperature automatically. Induction heats the whole pot for more even rice. Pressure induction gives the softest, stickiest texture.
  • Size: 3-cup models suit one or two people. 5.5-cup (Zojirushi) or 6-cup uncooked / 12-cup cooked (Cuckoo) models suit families.
  • Rice style: Japanese short-grain and Korean sticky rice benefit most from pressure. Jasmine and long-grain fans should look for a dedicated menu.
  • Bread machine size: 2-lb machines like the Virtuoso Plus suit families. 1-lb machines like the Maestro and Mini suit small homes.
  • Healthy courses: the Virtuoso Plus, Maestro and Cuisinart all offer gluten-free settings.
Gift tip: pair a rice cooker with a bag of premium Japanese short-grain rice, or a bread maker with good bread flour and a bread knife.
