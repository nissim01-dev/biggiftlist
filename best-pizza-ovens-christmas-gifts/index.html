Gas, wood or electric: which pizza oven to gift?

  • Gas (propane): the easiest outdoor choice. Turn a dial, wait 15–25 minutes, bake. Best for most people, especially beginners.
  • Wood or charcoal: the most flavor and fun, but the fire needs tending. Multi-fuel Ooni Karu ovens can add a gas burner (sold separately) later.
  • Electric outdoor: plug-in simple for patios with an outlet. Ovens like the Ninja Artisan don't get as hot as gas or wood, but they're very forgiving.
  • Indoor countertop: for apartments and year-round pizza. They take counter space, so check the room first.
  • Size: 12-inch ovens are compact and portable; 16-inch and larger ovens are better for families, parties and roasting.
  • Don't forget: outdoor ovens need a sturdy, heat-safe table, and gas ovens need a propane tank. A peel and an infrared thermometer make great add-ons.
Gift tip: make it a "pizza night kit": wrap the oven with a peel, a bag of 00 flour, a jar of good tomatoes and the infrared thermometer, and plan the first pizza night together.

FAQ

What is the best pizza oven to give as a gift?

For most people, a gas Ooni like the Koda 16 (or the compact Koda 12) is the easiest and most reliable choice. Wood-fire fans should look at the Ooni Karu 12 or Karu 2, and the Ninja Artisan is the simplest plug-in option.

Can you use a pizza oven indoors?

Only electric ovens made for indoor use, such as the Ooni Volt 2, Chefman, Cuisinart, Current Model P and BIG HORN countertop ovens. Never use gas or wood pizza ovens indoors.

How hot does a pizza oven get?

Gas and wood Ooni ovens reach about 950°F; the Ninja Artisan reaches 700°F; indoor countertop ovens on this list reach roughly 700–850°F.

Is a pizza oven worth it?

For people who love pizza or cooking outdoors, yes: high-heat ovens make crisp, leopard-spotted crusts in minutes, and many also roast vegetables, steak and bread.
