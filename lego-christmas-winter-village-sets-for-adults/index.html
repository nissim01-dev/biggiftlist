How to pick a LEGO Christmas set

A few quick tips before you buy:

  • Starting a village: Holiday Main Street or Santa's Post Office make the best first pieces. Add one new building each year.
  • For families: Santa's Visit and the Festive Gingerbread House are easy to build together and full of play details.
  • For movie fans: the Home Alone house is a big, detailed build with gags in every room.
  • Bigger town: modular buildings like Tudor Corner and the Jazz Club fit next to Winter Village sets on a long shelf, though they're not snowy.
  • Retired sets: several Winter Village sets are retired and sold by third-party sellers. Check the seller, ratings and return policy before buying.
Gift tip: add a roll of white felt or a snow blanket for the display. A LEGO village looks twice as festive on 'snow'.

FAQ

What is the best LEGO Christmas set for adults?

Holiday Main Street (10308) is the favorite centerpiece. Santa's Post Office (10339) is the newer pick with a light-up hot-air balloon, and Santa's Visit (10293) is the coziest Christmas Eve scene.

Is the LEGO Winter Village still made?

LEGO adds new holiday sets most years, but many older Winter Village sets are retired. They're still on Amazon, often from third-party sellers, so check the seller first.

Which LEGO Christmas set lights up?

Santa's Post Office has a light-up hot-air balloon burner and Santa's Visit has a tree with a light brick. The Home Alone house has a glowing basement furnace.

Is there a small LEGO Christmas gift?
