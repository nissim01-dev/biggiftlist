How to pick the right retro LEGO set

A few quick tips before you buy:

  • Match the memory: a photographer will love the Polaroid, a gamer the Game Boy, a writer the Typewriter. The more personal the nostalgia, the bigger the reaction.
  • Display or quick build: Ideas and Icons sets are big display pieces for adults. The Creator 3 in 1 sets are small, quick and rebuildable into other models.
  • Pair big and small: a display set plus the Retro Telephone or Retro Camera makes a great main gift and stocking stuffer combo.
  • Check the seller: several retro sets are retiring and sold by third-party sellers shipped by Amazon. Check today's price, stock and the return window before you order.
Gift tip: the Creator Retro Telephone and the Game Boy are selling thousands of units a month, and retiring sets like the Typewriter, Ship in a Bottle and Jazz Quartet have limited stock. Order early.
