How to choose a robot vacuum and mop combo

  • Dock type matters most. A basic dock just charges. A self-empty dock holds weeks of dirt. An all-in-one or omni dock also washes and dries mop pads, which is the real time-saver.
  • Mop style: spinning pads scrub well; roller mops (eufy E25, Roomba 705X) keep cleaning the roller as they go. Mops that lift protect carpets.
  • Navigation: LiDAR mapping is now standard. Camera-based AI obstacle avoidance helps with cords, socks and pet accidents.
  • Suction numbers (Pa) are useful within a brand but not a perfect comparison across brands.
  • Running costs: budget for dust bags, mop pads and filters every few months.
  • Dock size: all-in-one docks are tall. Measure the spot before you buy.

FAQ

Is a robot vacuum and mop combo worth it?

For homes with hard floors, yes. Modern combos vacuum and mop in one run, and docks that wash and dry the mops make them almost maintenance-free.

Roomba vs roborock: which is better?

roborock usually offers more features for the money, while iRobot's Roombas are known for simple setup. Both brands have strong 2026 models in this guide.

Do robot mops work on carpet?

Most 2026 combos lift their mops when they detect carpet, and some avoid carpet while mopping. Check the listing if you have lots of rugs.

How long do robot vacuums last?

Typically several years with regular brush, filter and mop replacements. Buying from Amazon or a major brand makes warranty support easier.
