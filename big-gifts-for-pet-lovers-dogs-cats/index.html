How to pick a big gift for a pet lover

A few quick tips before you buy:

  • Ask about the pet first: the cat's weight matters for self-cleaning litter boxes, and the dog's length matters for strollers and beds.
  • Check ongoing costs: some litter boxes use their own trays or bags, and some pet cameras have optional paid plans. The Furbo 360° Dog Camera's core features work without one.
  • Give pets time: many cats need a week or two to get used to a new litter box. Owners usually leave the old one out at first.
  • Measure the space: big cat trees and orthopedic beds take real floor space, so check the room first.
Gift tip: pair a big gift with a small one for the pet: a Furbo plus a bag of their favorite treats, or a cat tree with a new toy hanging from the top.
