How to pick the right craft machine gift

Match the machine to what they make most:

  • Vinyl, cards and shirts: Cricut Explore 4. It covers most hobby projects at a lower price than the Maker.
  • Fabric, felt and thin wood: Cricut Maker 4. It works with optional blades for tougher materials.
  • Monograms and patches: Brother SE700. It sews and embroiders in one machine.
  • Stickers and labels in a small space: Cricut Joy Xtra, which prints and then cuts.
  • Cricut vs Silhouette: Cricut's Design Space is easier to learn and has a huge community. Silhouette Studio is more flexible and doesn't need a subscription for most projects.
  • Budget for supplies: vinyl, iron-on, mats, blades, thread and blank mugs are ongoing costs. Wrapping a starter pack of materials with the machine makes the gift usable on Christmas morning.
Gift tip: a cutting machine plus the Cricut EasyPress SE makes a complete "make your own shirts" gift. Add a few sheets of iron-on vinyl and plain tees so they can finish their first project on Christmas Day.

FAQ

Which Cricut is best for a beginner?

The Cricut Joy Xtra is the easiest and most compact. If they plan to make shirts, decals and cards regularly, the Explore 4 is a better long-term buy, and it's still simple to learn.

Cricut Maker 4 or Explore 4?

Pick the Explore 4 for vinyl, iron-on and paper. Pick the Maker 4 if they want to cut fabric, felt or thin wood, because it supports the extra blades those materials need.

Do Cricut machines need a subscription?

No. The machines work with the free version of Design Space, but Cricut Access unlocks a much bigger image and font library for a monthly or yearly fee. Some bundles include bonus digital content.

Is Silhouette Cameo better than Cricut?

Neither is better for everyone. Silhouette is quieter and its software is more flexible without a subscription, while Cricut is easier for beginners and has more tutorials and ready-made projects.

What is the best embroidery machine for a beginner?

The Brother SE700 is a popular first choice because it also sews. If they already own a sewing machine, the embroidery-only Brother PE545 does the same 4x4 designs.
