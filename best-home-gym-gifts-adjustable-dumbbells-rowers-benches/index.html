How to pick the right home gym gift

Match the gift to the person and the space they have:

  • Strength first: adjustable dumbbells give the most workout per square foot. PowerBlock grows with the lifter; NordicTrack stores neatly in trays.
  • Add a bench: a bench unlocks presses and rows. The FLYBIRD folds away; the MAJOR FITNESS is for heavy garage lifting.
  • Cardio: rowers work the whole body with low impact. The WaterRower is the looker; the Sunny rowers are compact and easy to store.
  • Busy desk worker: a walking pad adds steps during calls without a workout slot.
  • Shipping: heavy items can ship slowly in December, and the WaterRower lists a 10 to 11 day wait. Order early.
