How to pick the right Kindle gift

  • Most people: a Paperwhite. The 7-inch screen, warm light and waterproofing are the sweet spot. Choose the 2024 model for proven reviews or the newest model for the thinner, lighter design.
  • Signature Edition adds: an auto-adjusting light, 32GB of storage and no lock-screen ads. The new aluminum Signature also adds double-tap page turns.
  • Color or black and white? Colorsoft shows covers, comics and highlights in soft, paper-like color. For novels, black and white is crisper and cheaper.
  • Scribe is for writing: pick it only if they'll take notes, mark up PDFs or journal. For reading alone, a Paperwhite is lighter and much cheaper.
  • Kids' versions: they cost a little more but include a cover, 12 months of Amazon Kids+ and a 2-year worry-free guarantee that replaces a broken Kindle.
  • Check the cover fits: covers are model-specific. Match the cover to the exact Kindle and year you're gifting.
Gift tip: wrap a Kindle with a matching cover and a Kindle Unlimited or Amazon gift card for their first books, or load a few favorites to their account so it's ready to read on Christmas morning.

FAQ

What is the best Kindle to gift for Christmas 2026?

For most readers, the Kindle Paperwhite. The 2024 model has thousands of strong reviews, and the newest model is thinner and lighter with up to 12 weeks of battery. Go Signature Edition for no ads and an auto-adjusting light.

Kindle Paperwhite or Colorsoft: which should I gift?

Pick Colorsoft if they read comics, graphic novels or cookbooks, or love colored highlights. For mostly novels, the Paperwhite's black-and-white screen is crisper and costs less.

Is the Kindle Scribe worth it as a gift?

Yes, if they'll write on it. The Scribe's 11-inch screen and included pen are great for notes, PDFs and journaling, and its AI notebook can summarize handwriting. For reading only, a Paperwhite is the better gift.

Which Kindle is best for kids?

The Kindle Paperwhite Kids for most children: a bigger waterproof screen and a warm light, plus a cover, 12 months of Amazon Kids+ and a 2-year worry-free guarantee. The regular Kindle Kids is the budget option, and Colorsoft Kids adds color.

Do Kindles have ads?

The basic Kindle and Paperwhite show ads on the lock screen. Signature Edition, Scribe and the kids' models don't.
