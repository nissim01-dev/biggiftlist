How to pick the right LEGO set for an 8 to 12 year old

Start with what they already love, then check the age on the box:

  • Star Wars fans: the Imperial Star Destroyer and the Razor Crest are both 10+ and built for 'flying' around the room.
  • Gamers: Minecraft sets turn the game into play scenes. The Ender Dragon is the boss fight; the Deep Dark Battle has the Warden reveal.
  • Younger builders (7 to 9): the City Passenger Airplane, the Airport and the remote-control train are easy to build and great for pretend play.
  • Future engineers: a Technic motorcycle or race car teaches gears, steering and suspension.
  • SMART Play: the SMART Play Millennium Falcon needs the LEGO SMART Brick from an All-in-One set (sold separately) for lights and sounds.
  • Third-party sellers: a few popular sets are sold by third-party sellers. Check the seller and the return policy before buying.
Gift tip: wrap a small LEGO sorting tray or a shelf with the box. Big sets are more fun when the pieces don't end up all over the floor.

FAQ

What is the best big LEGO set for a 10 year old?

For Star Wars fans, the Imperial Star Destroyer (75394) or the Razor Crest (75447). For gamers, the Minecraft Ender Dragon and End Ship. For vehicle lovers, the Technic Kawasaki Ninja H2R.

Which LEGO set is best for an 8 year old?

The Minecraft Ender Dragon and End Ship, the Deep Dark Battle, the City Airport and the NINJAGO Thunderfang Dragon are all rated 8+ and full of play features.

Is there a LEGO set that moves on its own?

Yes. The LEGO City Express Passenger Train drives with a remote or the LEGO Powered Up app and has working headlights.

Does the SMART Play Millennium Falcon include the SMART Brick?

No. The SMART Brick comes in the All-in-One sets, sold separately. The Falcon still works as a regular play ship without it.
