FAQ

Is the Ninja SLUSHi worth it?

Owners love it for parties and summer, and it makes a true slush texture without ice. It's big, though, so make sure the recipient has counter or storage space.

Can the Ninja CREAMi make slushies?

The CREAMi XL Deluxe has slushie and frozen drink programs, but the SLUSHi is far better for real slushies because it chills drinks on demand.

Which CREAMi is best?

The original 7-in-1 is the best value. The Scoop & Swirl adds soft serve, and the XL Deluxe suits big families.
