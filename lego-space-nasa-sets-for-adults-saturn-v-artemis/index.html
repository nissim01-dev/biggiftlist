How to pick the right LEGO space set

Match the set to the fan and the space they have:

  • The ultimate display: the Saturn V is about a meter tall. The Artemis SLS and Shuttle Carrier Aircraft are big too, so measure the shelf first.
  • Moon history buff: the Apollo 11 Lunar Lander and the Lunar Roving Vehicle make a great pair on one shelf.
  • Stargazer: the Hubble Space Telescope or the Milky Way wall art fits the person who owns a telescope.
  • Building with kids: the Technic Artemis rocket (9+), Planet Earth and Moon in Orbit and Mars Perseverance (10+) are friendly for younger builders.
  • Third-party sellers: some popular older sets are sold mostly by third-party sellers. Check the seller and the return policy before buying.
Gift tip: pair a LEGO rocket with a launch-day plan: wrap it with a note to watch the next big launch stream together.
