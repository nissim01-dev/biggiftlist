What pet owners should look for

  • Self-emptying dock: fur fills a robot's bin fast. A self-empty base is the single most useful feature for pet homes.
  • Tangle-resistant brushes: look for rubber or anti-tangle brushrolls, especially with long-haired pets.
  • Obstacle avoidance: camera-based AI avoidance helps the robot steer around toys, bowls and accidents.
  • Schedule daily runs: short daily cleans keep shedding under control far better than one weekly run.
  • Filters: for allergy sufferers, check for a high-efficiency filter and replace it regularly.
Pet tip: run the robot when pets are calm or out on a walk the first few times; most dogs and cats get used to it within a week.

FAQ

What is the best robot vacuum for pet hair?

The eufy E25 is a strong all-round pick, and Techlicious named it a pet-hair value in October 2026. On a budget, Shark's self-emptying robots are popular with pet owners.

Do robot vacuums avoid pet poop?

Models with camera-based AI obstacle avoidance, like the dreame X60 Max Ultra and roborock Saros 20, are designed to recognize and avoid pet waste, but no robot is perfect. Check the floor before a run.

Is a self-emptying robot vacuum worth it with pets?

Yes. Pet hair fills robot bins quickly, and a self-empty dock means emptying a bag or bin every few weeks instead of every day.

Can robot vacuums handle long hair?

Most 2026 models have anti-tangle brushes, but long hair can still wrap around side brushes. Expect to clean the brushes occasionally.
