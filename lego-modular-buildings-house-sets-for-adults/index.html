How to pick a LEGO modular building

A few quick tips before you buy:

  • Modular or stand-alone: the Police Station, Bookshop, Assembly Square, Parisian Restaurant, Jazz Club and Tudor Corner connect with each other. Cabins, villages and book nooks stand alone.
  • Measure the shelf: most modulars are about 10 inches wide and deep. A street of three needs a shelf around 30 inches long.
  • Retired sets: the Police Station, Bookshop, Assembly Square and Parisian Restaurant are retired and sold by third-party sellers. Check the seller, ratings and return policy before buying.
  • Add light: modular interiors look great with a LED light kit, which also makes a nice add-on gift.
Gift tip: start a tradition. Gift one modular building every Christmas, and in a few years they have a whole LEGO street that grew with the family.

FAQ

What are LEGO modular buildings?

They are a series of detailed LEGO buildings for adults, each with a full interior, that connect side by side to build a city street. The Jazz Club, Tudor Corner, Police Station and Bookshop are all part of the series.

Which LEGO modular building should I start with?

The Jazz Club (10312) is a great current modular to start with. If they want a classic, the retired Police Station (10278) and Bookshop (10270) are fan favorites and still on Amazon from third-party sellers.

What is the biggest LEGO modular building?

Assembly Square (10255) has 4,002 pieces, with a bakery, florist, café, dentist and dance studio in one building.

Are there LEGO house sets that aren't modular?

Yes. The A-Frame Cabin, Italian Riviera, Autumn Cottage Garden, Bookstore Book Nook and Home Alone house are stand-alone house sets for adults.
