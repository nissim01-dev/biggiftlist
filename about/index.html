About The Big Gift List

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

The Big Gift List is a small, independent gift-guide site for the "big one" under the tree: the telescope, the stand mixer, the robot vacuum or the photo frame that becomes a family favorite. It's a sister site of Cozy Cup Corner.

What we cover

  • Stargazing: beginner, kids' and app-controlled smart telescopes.
  • Dream kitchen: KitchenAid and other stand mixers, viral Ninja appliances, Breville and Vitamix.
  • Smart cleaning: robot vacuums, mop combos and pet-hair specialists.
  • Hobbies: big LEGO sets for adults and golf tech like launch monitors, rangefinders and GPS watches.
  • Gifts by person: dad, mom, grandparents and the whole family.

How we choose products

We research each category using manufacturer specs, Amazon listings with long sales histories and large numbers of ratings, Amazon Best Sellers rankings, and what independent reviewers and owner communities consistently report. Every product link is checked against its Amazon listing before publishing so the item you land on is the one we describe. We prefer listings shipped or sold by Amazon.

We don't run a test lab and we don't pretend to: the "What owners like" and "Common complaints" boxes summarize reviewer and owner consensus, and we write all descriptions ourselves rather than copying reviews.

We don't show exact prices because they change constantly, especially between Black Friday and Christmas. Instead we give a general price band and link to Amazon for the current price.
